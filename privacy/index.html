Privacy policy

Rallr is a volleyball rotation planner and match tracker for coaches. No account or login is needed.

Your team data

Team names, player numbers, optional player names and roles, lineups, match scores and notes are stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, this data syncs through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. The Rallr developer cannot see it.

Sharing with players is optional

When you share a team, a copy goes to Apple CloudKit’s public database under a join code. By default, it includes player numbers and first names only. Full names are included only if you turn them on.

Players who join enter nothing about themselves. Choosing “Stop Sharing” or “Reset Code” deletes the shared copy.

Anonymous usage events

Rallr uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous usage events, such as “match started.” These events are not tied to a name, email or identity. No events are collected in the player view.

There is no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat checks whether Pro is unlocked using an anonymous ID and purchase records. The Rallr developer never sees your payment details.

Photos

Rallr asks to save a photo only when you choose “Save Image.” It can add images to your photo library, but cannot read the library.

Children and deleting data

Rallr is for coaches. Player names are optional.

  • To remove a player, delete that player from the team.
  • To delete a shared team copy, choose “Stop Sharing” or “Reset Code.”
  • To remove all local data, delete the app. You can remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

How long data is kept

Team data is kept until you delete it. The shared copy is kept until you stop sharing it.
