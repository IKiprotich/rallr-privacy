Privacy policy

Rallr is a volleyball rotation planner and match tracker for coaches. No account or login is needed.

Your team data

Your teams, players, lineups, matches, stats, drawings and notes are stored on your device.

If you use iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud account. The Rallr developer cannot see them.

Sharing with players is optional

When you share a team, a copy is stored on Apple’s iCloud servers, and anyone with the join code can view it. It includes player numbers and first names only, unless you turn on full names. Another coach with the code can copy it.

Players who join enter nothing about themselves. “Stop Sharing” or “Reset Code” deletes the shared copy.

Backup files

A backup file is made only when you export one, and it goes only where you save or send it.

Anonymous usage events

Rallr uses TelemetryDeck to count anonymous app events, such as “match started.” Events use a random device ID and are never tied to your name, email or identity. None are collected in the player view.

There is no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat checks whether Pro is unlocked using an anonymous ID. The Rallr developer never sees your payment details.

Photos

Rallr only adds an image to your photo library when you choose “Save Image.” It cannot read your library.

Children and deleting data

Rallr is for coaches. Player names are optional.

  • To remove a player, delete that player from the team.
  • To delete a shared team copy, choose “Stop Sharing” or “Reset Code.”
  • To remove all local data, delete the app. You can remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

How long data is kept

Team data is kept until you delete it. The shared copy is kept until you stop sharing it.
