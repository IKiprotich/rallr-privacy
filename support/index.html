Common questions

How do I restore purchases?

Open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, then select Rallr to cancel your subscription.

How do I stop sharing a team?

Open Team > Share with Players > Stop Sharing.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove local data. Remove the iCloud copy in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.
